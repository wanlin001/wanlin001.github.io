On 9 October 1963 roughly 270 million cubic metres of Monte Toc slid into the reservoir behind
what was then the tallest dam in the world. The dam itself held. The wave did not — it
overtopped the crest by about 250 m and destroyed Longarone in the valley below.

{% include photos.html dir="/images/travel/vajont" files="20260516_152714_monte-toc.jpg" size="tall" %}

The slide surface is still bare after sixty years. From the valley road it reads as a single
pale sheet across the whole flank of the mountain.

{% include photos.html dir="/images/travel/vajont" files="20260516_184330_longarone.jpg" size="tall" %}

Seen from Longarone station, the gorge frames the dam wall almost exactly. Standing here it is
very easy to understand what happened, and hard to understand how it was allowed to.
