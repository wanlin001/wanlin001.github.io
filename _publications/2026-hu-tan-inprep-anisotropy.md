Mantle flow shapes crystallographic fabric, which is recorded as seismic anisotropy. By tuning
model parameters until the predicted anisotropy matches observations, tectonic evolution and
mantle circulation beneath the Manila–Taiwan–Ryukyu system can be inferred from seismic data.
The abstract linked above was presented at EGU 2026.
