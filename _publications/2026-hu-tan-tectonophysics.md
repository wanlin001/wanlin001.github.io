Surface deformation in the mudstone-rich fold-thrust belt of southwestern Taiwan can be
explained either by fault geometry or by the weakness of the mudstone itself. By combining
thermal-mechanical finite-element modelling with crustal-scale geodetic observations, this study
separates the two contributions and provides mechanistic constraints for regional seismic hazard
assessment.
