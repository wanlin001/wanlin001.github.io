A popular-science piece for the quarterly *Geology (Ti-Chi)*. In 2022 I was in Aceh searching
for non-volcanic tremor along the Great Sumatran Fault, and took the chance to spend time in
Banda Aceh.

It opens with the generator barge the tsunami carried kilometres inland, the mosques, the mass
graves; then the tectonic setting of the 2004 Mw 9.1 earthquake; then how disaster is
remembered and displayed. A geological hazard is never only a scientific problem — it is also
a question of how a place chooses to remember and rebuild.
