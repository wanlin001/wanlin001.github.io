The 2006 Pingtung offshore earthquake doublet ruptured both normal and strike-slip faults within
the northern Manila subduction zone. Joint interpretation of the earthquake source with
800-km-scale geodynamic models constrains the rheology of thinned continental lithosphere and
demonstrates strong intraslab stress heterogeneity.
